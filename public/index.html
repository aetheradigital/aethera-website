FAQ

Questions, answered.

Straight answers about what we do, what it costs and how working together starts.

What does Aethera Digital do?

Aethera Digital is a boutique creative, marketing and digital-systems studio. We cover eight disciplines (brand development and design, websites, SEO and AI search visibility, advertising and funnels, content, social and video, CRM and back office, portals and dashboards, and reporting and strategy) and connect them into one customer journey.

How much does the Startup Package cost?

The Startup Package is $2,250 a month, month-to-month with no long contract. It includes up to a 5-page website, one ad platform where it fits, a monthly report and one monthly strategy meeting. Ad spend, software, paid assets and third-party costs are billed separately.

Can I hire you for a single project?

Yes. You can start with an individual project with a clear scope (a brand, a website, a campaign or a dashboard), launch brand, site, search and lead follow-up together in a combined launch engagement, or work with us month-to-month as an ongoing digital partner.

How do you help a business show up in AI search?

We structure your site so search engines and AI assistants like ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews and Perplexity can read it, understand it and cite it: clear answers to the questions your customers ask, structured data that names your business and services, and pages AI crawlers can access. Then we track whether your brand appears in AI answers and report on it every month.

Who do you work with?

New businesses that need to show up and follow up from day one, and growing businesses that want their brand, marketing and back office working as one connected system.

What happens after I send an inquiry?

We reply within one business day with next steps. Tell us where you are and where you want to be, and we’ll come back with a plan, not a pitch.
