Privacy Policy

Effective November 21, 2026

This policy explains what information Aethera Digital (“Aethera,” “we,” “us”) collects through aetheradigital.com, our payment pages, our quote pages and the Aethera client portal at client.aetheradigital.com, how we use it, and the choices you have. Questions are always welcome at hello@aetheradigital.com.

Information we collect

  • What you send us. Your name, email, company and the details you include when you fill out our contact form, email us, or meet with us.
  • Client account information. For clients, the contact details on your account, plus the projects, files, approvals, reports, quotes and messages we share with you in the portal.
  • Quotes and signatures. When you accept a quote, the name and email you sign with, the options you choose, the time you signed and the version of our Terms of Service you agreed to.
  • Billing. Invoice and payment records, such as amounts, dates and status. Card and bank details are entered directly into Stripe’s secure fields and are never stored on our servers.
  • Portal sign-in and activity. When you use the client portal, we record sign-ins, downloads, approvals and payments on your account, along with the IP address and a general device description (for example, “Chrome on Mac”). You can see this activity in the portal.
  • Website usage. Our public website uses Google Tag Manager and Google Analytics to understand how visitors find and use the site, such as pages viewed and the general region and type of device.
  • Marketing performance data. If you are a client and give us access, we pull data from your own accounts, such as Google Search Console and Google Analytics, to prepare your reports.

How we use it

  • To respond to inquiries, prepare quotes and deliver the work you hire us for.
  • To send invoices, process payments and keep accurate business records.
  • To run the client portal, including emailing you secure sign-in links and showing your account activity.
  • To prepare client reports and keep projects on track. We use AI tools, including Anthropic’s Claude, to help draft reports, review the performance of sites we build, and organize action items from client emails and meeting notes. A member of our team reviews this work.
  • To improve our website and services, and to protect our systems from misuse.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with anyone for their own advertising.

Who helps us

We rely on a small number of service providers who process information on our behalf and only as needed to provide their service:

  • Stripe for payments and invoicing
  • Google Workspace for email, documents, spreadsheets and meeting notes, and Google Analytics for website analytics
  • Render for hosting our website, portal and database
  • Cloudflare for storing files shared in the client portal
  • Anthropic for the AI assistance described above

We may also share information if required by law, to protect our rights or the safety of others, or as part of a sale or reorganization of our business.

Cookies

The client portal and our internal tools use essential cookies to keep you signed in. Portal sessions last up to 30 days or until you sign out. Our public website also uses analytics cookies set by Google. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings; the portal needs its sign-in cookie to work.

How we protect it

The portal signs you in with single-use links that expire after 15 minutes, so there is no password to steal. Each client account can see only its own information, connections are encrypted, and account activity is logged. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to keep your information safe and limit access to the people who need it.

How long we keep it

We keep client records, quotes, signed agreements and billing records for as long as we work together and afterward as needed for legal, tax and accounting purposes. We keep inquiries for as long as they are useful for following up. You can ask us to delete information we no longer need to keep.

Your choices

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your personal information, or to stop contacting you, by emailing hello@aetheradigital.com. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under local law, and we will honor them as required.

Children

Our services are for businesses and are not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If we update this policy, we will post the new version here with a new effective date. If the changes are significant, we will let clients know.
